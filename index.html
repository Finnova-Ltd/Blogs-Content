About Us

Digital Access & Dignity for All Australians

Finnova Ltd is an Australian registered charity, ISO 27001:2022 certified organisation, and Public Benevolent Institution (PBI) working to close the digital divide. We help people locked out of the digital world — through poverty, disability, homelessness, family violence, financial hardship or social isolation — get safe, confident access to online services.

What you need to know

📋

About the Census

Every 5 years, Australia counts every person and household. The data shapes decisions on schools, hospitals, transport and community services for the next decade.

❓

Census questions

Questions cover your address, household, personal details, cultural background, work, education and housing. Each one helps plan the services your community needs.

🔒

Keeping your information safe

Your privacy is protected by law. Your name and address are separated from your answers and never published. No one can access your personal information unlawfully.

🆘

Support available

Help is available in 12+ languages, for people with disability, and for those experiencing homelessness. Finnova can connect you to the right support.

⚠️

Avoiding scams

No official organisation will ever ask for your bank details, passwords or money to complete the Census. If something feels wrong, stop and ask Finnova.

Empirical Research & National Index

Measuring Australia's Digital Divide

Key metrics from the Australian Digital Inclusion Index (ADII) research.

20.6%
Digitally Excluded

Around 1 in 5 Australians are excluded or highly excluded from basic digital access, rising to 66.5% for seniors aged 75+ and 45.2% in public housing.

3.5 Million
Computers to Landfill

3.5 million computers end up in Australian landfill every year while millions of vulnerable citizens lack fit-for-purpose hardware.

9.7%
Mobile-Only Users

Almost 10% of Australians rely solely on smartphones, making job applications, study, and myGov document uploads extremely difficult.

10.5 pts
First Nations Digital Gap

The national inclusion gap for First Nations people widens to 22.8 points in very remote communities where 40.9% are excluded.

⚖️ Finnova Ltd – 2026 Census Administrative Support Tool Disclaimer

1. Nature of Service: This digital utility is owned and operated by Finnova Ltd (ABN: 55687130767). This tool is an independent administrative aid designed to assist aged care facility managers in aggregating and organising internal data ahead of the 2026 Census. Finnova Ltd is a registered charity and is not an official partner, agent, or representative of the Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS).

2. Data Lodgement and Compliance: This tool does not submit data directly to the ABS. The facility manager retains sole legal responsibility for entering, verifying, and submitting all final resident data directly into the official, secure ABS online portal using the official credentials provided by the government. Finnova Ltd accepts no liability for non-compliance, late submissions, or data entry errors made on the official Census website.

3. Data Security and Privacy: Finnova Ltd values privacy and operates in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). Any data uploaded to this portal is encrypted in transit and at rest. Finnova Ltd does not store, sell, or share resident data. All uploaded administrative files and data fields are automatically and permanently purged from our servers 48 hours after Census Night (Thursday, 13 August 2026). Facility managers are strictly advised not to share account passwords or sensitive clinical medical files through the chat feature.

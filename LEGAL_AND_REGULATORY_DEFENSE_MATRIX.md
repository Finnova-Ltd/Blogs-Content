# Legal & Regulatory Defense Matrix: Australian Mortgage Broking & AI Compliance

## Executive Summary & Statutory Framework
This document establishes the official regulatory defense protocols, compliance boundaries, and deterministic refusal/re-engagement mechanisms for **EZ Mortgage Broker** and the **Friday AI Conversational Engine**.

Operating in the Australian credit industry requires strict adherence to:
* **National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009 (NCCP Act)**
* **Statutory Best Interests Duty (BID)** — NCCP Act Part 3-5A (Sections 158EA–158JE) & **ASIC Regulatory Guide 273 (RG 273)**
* **Responsible Lending Conduct Obligations** — NCCP Act Part 3-2, Div 3 & **ASIC Regulatory Guide 209 (RG 209)**
* **Australian Securities and Investments Commission Act 2001 (ASIC Act)** — Section 12DA (Misleading & Deceptive Conduct)
* **Competition and Consumer Act 2010 / Australian Consumer Law (ACL)** — Section 18
* **Privacy Act 1988 (Cth)** & **Australian Privacy Principles (APPs)**
* **Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2006 (AML/CTF Act)**

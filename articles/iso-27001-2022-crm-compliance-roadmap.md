# ISO 27001:2022 certification for CRM platforms: Implementing Annex A security controls

> **Executive Brief**: A pragmatic roadmap for Australian businesses implementing ISO 27001:2022 Information Security Management Systems (ISMS), data retention policies, and immutable logging across CRM databases.

Achieving ISO 27001:2022 certification demonstrates to enterprise clients and government regulators that your organization adheres to globally recognized security controls. The updated standard places heightened emphasis on cloud service security, threat intelligence, and data leakage prevention.

For CRM environments storing sensitive participant or customer records, key Annex A controls include database encryption at rest (AES-256), cryptographic key rotation, granular role-based permissions, and immutable audit logging.

PRO CRM builds ISO 27001:2022 compliance directly into our platform architecture, providing Australian organizations with turnkey policies, security registers, and audit evidence artifacts.

# Edge AI and small language models: Lowering inference latency in enterprise CRM workflows

> **Executive Brief**: How Australian development teams leverage quantized small language models (SLMs) running on Cloudflare Workers and edge nodes to deliver instantaneous form extraction and smart CRM routing.

While frontier large language models (LLMs) excel at creative synthesis, their inference latency and token costs make them impractical for high-frequency transactional CRM tasks like field validation, intent classification, and inbound email parsing.

Deploying fine-tuned Small Language Models (SLMs) such as Mistral-NeMo and Llama-3-8B on edge compute networks reduces response times from 3 seconds to under 200 milliseconds, all while processing sensitive PII within local Australian geographic boundaries.

PRO CRM utilizes edge intelligence to triage inbound customer inquiries, extract unstructured data from PDF invoices, and trigger instant webhook automations before requests ever reach origin servers.

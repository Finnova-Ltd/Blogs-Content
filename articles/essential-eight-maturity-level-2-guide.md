# Achieving Essential Eight Maturity Level 2: A practical engineering blueprint for Australian SaaS

> **Executive Brief**: Step-by-step guidance on implementing application whitelisting, daily immutable cloud backups, MFA enforcement, and patch automation to meet Australian government cyber standards.

The Australian Signals Directorate (ASD) and ACSC Essential Eight framework defines strict criteria for mitigating targeted cyber attacks. Reaching Maturity Level 2 requires automated patch management within 48 hours for critical vulnerabilities and enforced MFA across all cloud access points.

For software providers and CRM operators, compliance involves sandboxing untrusted macros, restricting local administrative privileges, and maintaining immutable write-once-read-many (WORM) daily backups with tested rapid recovery procedures.

PRO CRM embeds Essential Eight controls into every client build, delivering automated infrastructure-as-code audits, continuous log forwarding to Australian SIEM endpoints, and ISO 27001:2022 certified governance.

# APRA CPS 234 & Privacy Act Enforcement: Managing Boardroom Liability and Systemic Cyber Deficiencies

> **Executive Brief**: With Australian regulators issuing multi-million dollar penalties for information security failures, executive boards must mandate immutable logging, penetration testing, and continuous audit readiness.

Australian regulatory bodies, including APRA, ASIC, and the OAIC, have significantly escalated enforcement actions against financial institutions and digital platforms exhibiting substandard cybersecurity governance, with recent penalties exceeding $8 million.

APRA Prudential Standard CPS 234 demands that regulated entities define information security capabilities commensurate with the size and extent of cyber threats, maintain comprehensive testing schedules, and report security incidents within 72 hours.

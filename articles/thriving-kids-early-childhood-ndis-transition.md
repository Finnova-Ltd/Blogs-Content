# What Are the Hybrid Medicare and NDIS Billing Rules for the New Thriving Kids Program?

> **Executive Brief**: Australia's new Thriving Kids initiative creates alternative foundational pathways for children with developmental delay, combining Medicare MBS paediatric items with state grants and early intervention NDIS plans.

Australia's new Thriving Kids initiative creates alternative foundational support pathways for children with developmental delay, combining Medicare MBS paediatric items with state grants and early intervention NDIS plans across allied health clinics.

Key Program Requirements:
• Dual Funding Routing: Clinics must separate Medicare bulk-billed consultations from individualized NDIS capacity-building line items.
• Early Intervention Tiering: Foundational supports are accessible through community hubs prior to formal NDIS access decisions.
• Clinical Note Auditing: Speech and OT treatment plans require structured developmental milestone tracking aligned with DSS criteria.

# What Are the New Direct Care Minutes Compliance Deadlines for Australian Aged Care Operators?

> **Executive Brief**: Under the new Aged Care Act and Strengthened Quality Standards, Australian residential and in-home aged care providers must maintain legally auditable proof of mandatory direct care minute thresholds, 24/7 Registered Nurse coverage, and rapid Serious Incident Response Scheme (SIRS) escalations.

Under the new Aged Care Act and Strengthened Quality Standards, Australian residential and in-home aged care providers must maintain legally auditable proof of mandatory direct care minute thresholds, 24/7 Registered Nurse coverage, and rapid Serious Incident Response Scheme (SIRS) escalations.

### Key Regulatory Requirements & Operational Deadlines:

- Direct Care Minute Quotas: Residential facilities must deliver a mandatory average of 215 direct care minutes per resident per day, including at least 44 minutes provided directly by registered nurses.
- Shift-Level Electronic Verification: Manual rosters and spreadsheets are non-compliant; care hours must be authenticated through electronic time-attendance systems linked to clinical payroll ledgers.
- Strengthened SIRS Escalation: Unresolved clinical incidents, physical restraints, and restrictive practices must be escalated to the Quality and Safety Commission within 24 hours.
- Governing Body Liability: Company directors and executive leaders face enforceable statutory compliance duties and substantial civil penalties for falsified direct care logs.

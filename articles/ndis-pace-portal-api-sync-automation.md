# NDIS PACE system API integration: Automating bulk claiming, budget reconciliation & audits

> **Executive Brief**: The NDIA's nationwide PACE transition demands automated portal synchronization. Discover how connected CRM workflows eliminate manual claim rejections and keep providers 100% audit-ready.

The NDIA's nationwide transition to the PACE system introduces fundamental changes to participant endorsement rules, plan category structures, and billing line-item validations. Providers relying on manual portal entry face mounting claim backlogs and cash flow bottlenecks.

Direct API synchronization between PRO CRM and the NDIS Business-to-Government (B2G) gateway allows plan managers and support coordinators to validate support items against live price caps prior to submission, reducing claim rejection rates to below 0.5%.

Additionally, all service delivery notes, shift sign-offs, and eSigned service agreements are cryptographically bound to the participant's CRM timeline, providing instant evidentiary verification during Quality and Safeguards Commission reviews.

# How Will the 2026 NDIS Reform Bill and Anti-Fraud Taskforce Impact Disability Provider Billing?

> **Executive Brief**: The passage of the NDIS Amendment Bill 2026 and intensified Federal Police fraud investigations mandate stricter invoice line-item justification, price-cap adherence, and automated proof of service delivery for all registered providers.

The passage of the NDIS Amendment (Securing the NDIS) Bill 2026 and intensified Federal Police fraud taskforce operations mandate stricter invoice line-item justification, strict price-cap adherence, and automated proof of service delivery for all Australian disability service providers.

Critical Compliance Mandates for NDIS Providers:
• Mandatory Participant Endorsement: Bulk claims submitted without verified participant schedule agreements face automatic rejection in the NDIA PACE system.
• Fraud Taskforce Audit Triggers: Regulatory authorities are actively targeting duplicate claims, inflated travel line items, and unverified 1-on-1 support hours.
• B2G API Gateway Integration: Real-time digital transaction pipelines replace manual CSV spreadsheet uploads to guarantee evidentiary compliance.
• Tamper-Evident Sign-Offs: Support workers must record GPS-geofenced clock-ins and cryptographic participant signatures directly at the point of care.

# Offshore Contractor & Third-Party Access Security: Mitigating Supply Chain Vulnerabilities in Australian Enterprises

> **Executive Brief**: Recent high-profile Australian breach investigations highlight the extreme risk of unmonitored third-party vendor credentials. How zero-trust session recording and conditional access protect core systems.

Recent forensic investigations across the Australian energy and corporate sectors have demonstrated that external consultants, offshore integrators, and third-party service providers represent the soft underbelly of enterprise cyber defence.

Granting broad VPN or static credential access to third-party accounts bypasses internal perimeter controls. Once a contractor's laptop or overseas endpoint is compromised, attackers move laterally across internal Salesforce, SAP, and active directory systems undetected.

Eliminating supply chain exposure requires zero-trust conditional access policies. Organizations must enforce ephemeral, browser-isolated admin sessions, mandatory hardware token MFA, and unalterable cryptographic session video recordings for all outsourced development and administrative tasks.

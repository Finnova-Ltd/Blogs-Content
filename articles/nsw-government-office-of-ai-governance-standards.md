# How Can Australian Enterprises Comply with the NSW Government Office of AI Guidelines?

> **Executive Brief**: The establishment of the NSW Government Office of AI requires enterprises deploying autonomous agents and predictive models to implement documented risk assessments, algorithmic auditing, and human-in-the-loop oversight.

The establishment of the NSW Government Office of Artificial Intelligence requires Australian enterprises deploying autonomous agents and predictive models to implement documented risk assessments, algorithmic auditing, and human-in-the-loop oversight to ensure ethical data governance.

Mandatory Governance Benchmarks:
• Algorithmic Transparency: Automated decision systems affecting customer records must provide explainable, auditable reasoning traces.
• Bias & Risk Assessments: Organizations must perform continuous compliance testing against discriminatory predictive scoring.
• Human-in-the-Loop Gates: High-impact transactional triggers must require explicit staff approval before database commits.

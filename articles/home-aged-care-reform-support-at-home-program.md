# How Can Home Care Providers Prepare for the Support at Home Program and Assessment Overhauls?

> **Executive Brief**: The national transition to the Support at Home Program requires in-home aged care operators to replace legacy Home Care Packages (HCP) with single-tier budget management, automated intake routing, and specialized clinical tracking.

The national transition to the Support at Home Program requires Australian in-home aged care operators to replace legacy Home Care Packages (HCP) with single-tier budget management, automated intake routing, and specialized clinical tracking for over 154,000 awaiting seniors.

Key Operational Changes for Home Care Operators:
• Unified Assessment System: Single national assessment framework streamlines approvals and eliminates fragmented ACAT and RAS assessment channels.
• Real-Time Subsidy Burn-Rate Tracking: Care managers must monitor quarterly funding caps with itemized expenditure ledgers for each individual senior.
• Standardized Clinical Service Coding: In-home allied health, restorative therapy, and daily personal care must align with national clinical taxonomies.
• Transparent Client Statements: Monthly financial statements must be automatically generated and delivered with zero administrative overhead.

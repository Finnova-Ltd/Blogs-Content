# How Can Australian Organizations Ensure Data Provenance and Verifiable Citations in AI Knowledge Bases?

> **Executive Brief**: Generative AI responses in customer service and compliance reporting must be grounded on authenticated corporate data with cryptographic citations to eliminate hallucinations and legal liability.

Generative AI responses in customer service and compliance reporting must be grounded on authenticated corporate data with cryptographic citations to eliminate hallucinations, factual inaccuracies, and legal liability under Australian consumer protection laws.

Data Provenance Requirements:
• Zero-Copy Grounding: Models retrieve facts directly from internal data warehouses without copying records into unencrypted vector stores.
• Interactive Source Citations: Every AI answer generates clickable, authenticated references mapped to exact policy paragraph numbers.
• Human Editorial Review: High-visibility knowledge articles require one-click subject matter expert sign-off prior to publishing.

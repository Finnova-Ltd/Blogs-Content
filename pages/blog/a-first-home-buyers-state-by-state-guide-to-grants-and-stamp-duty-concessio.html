How Much Deposit Is Required for the First Home Guarantee in Victoria?

📅 18-Aug-2026 ⏱️ 2 min read ✍️ EZ Mortgage Research Desk
⚡ Direct Policy & Rate Summary

First home buyers in Victoria and Australia-wide can access the Home Guarantee Scheme (5% deposit with zero Lenders Mortgage Insurance) alongside state-based First Home Owner Grants ($10,000) and stamp duty exemptions under $600,000 thresholds.

Borrower Impact & Assessment Analysis:

  • ✓ Deposit Multipliers: Eligible buyers need as little as 5% genuine savings, avoiding tens of thousands in upfront LMI costs.
  • ✓ Government Price Caps: Property price caps apply per capital city and regional zone across all participating lenders.
  • ✓ Pre-Approval Crucial: Scheme allocation places fill rapidly; securing conditional pre-approval ensures your spot is reserved before auction day.

Superannuation & Home Loans: FHSSS $50k Withdrawal & SMSF Property Lending Rules

Maximize your deposit by releasing up to $50,000 via FHSSS tax concessions, or leverage your SMSF balance to purchase commercial and residential property with LRBA loans.

📅 19-Aug-2026 ⏱️ 5 min read ✍️ R BAKSHI (Principal Broker)

Maximize your deposit by releasing up to $50,000 via FHSSS tax concessions, or leverage your SMSF balance to purchase commercial and residential property with LRBA loans.

The First Home Super Saver Scheme (FHSSS) allows first home buyers in Australia to build a deposit inside their superannuation fund by making voluntary concessional (before-tax) and non-concessional (after-tax) contributions. Under updated ATO legislation, eligible individuals can release up to $15,000 per financial year, up to a maximum total of $50,000 per individual ($100,000 combined for couples).

Self-Managed Super Funds (SMSFs) can borrow money to purchase single acquirable real estate assets through a Limited Recourse Borrowing Arrangement (LRBA). In the event of loan default, the lender’s rights are strictly limited to the underlying security property and cannot touch other assets within your super fund.

📋 Key SMSF Lending Parameters for 2026

  • Maximum LVR: Up to 80% LVR for residential property; up to 75%–80% LVR for commercial property.
  • Minimum Fund Balance: Combined fund balance of $250,000+ recommended by ASIC for cost efficiency.
  • Liquidity Buffer: Lenders require 5% to 10% in liquid post-settlement cash buffers inside the fund.
  • Repayment Options: Principal & Interest or 5-year Interest-Only loan structures.

One of the most tax-efficient strategies for Australian business owners is purchasing their office, factory, or medical clinic through their SMSF and leasing it back to their own operating company at commercial market rates.

🛠️ SMSF Loan Checklist

  • Step 1: Trust Deed Verification: Ensure your SMSF Trust Deed explicitly authorizes borrowing under Section 67A of the SIS Act.
  • Step 2: Bare Trust Establishment: Establish a Bare Trust (Custodian Trust) and corporate trustee prior to executing any purchase contract.
  • Step 3: Pre-Approval: Submit member contribution histories and fund financial statements to accredited SMSF panel lenders.
  • Step 4: Settlement: Coordinate loan documentation between your SMSF accountant, solicitor, and mortgage broker.

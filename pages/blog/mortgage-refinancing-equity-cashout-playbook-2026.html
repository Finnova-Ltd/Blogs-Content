Mortgage Refinancing & Equity Cash Out: The Complete Australian Homeowner Playbook

Learn how to calculate your usable equity up to 80% LVR, consolidate high-interest debts, and switch lenders to reduce monthly repayments.

📅 19-Aug-2026 ⏱️ 5 min read ✍️ R BAKSHI (Principal Broker)

Learn how to calculate your usable equity up to 80% LVR, consolidate high-interest debts, and switch lenders to reduce monthly repayments.

Equity is the difference between the current market value of your property and the amount you owe on your mortgage. Australian lenders allow you to access usable equity up to 80% Loan-to-Value Ratio (LVR) without paying Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI).

Releasing equity can provide vital liquidity when structured into a separate sub-account rather than mixed with your home loan.

🎯 Strategic Equity Uses

  • Investment Property Deposit & Costs: Borrow 100% of purchase price + stamp duty with zero personal cash down.
  • Home Renovations: Add value to your primary residence or prepare for resale.
  • High-Interest Debt Consolidation: Replace 15%–22% credit card and personal loan rates with ~6% mortgage rates.
  • Working Capital Buffer: Secure standby liquidity for business expansion or investments.

⚡ Step-by-Step Refinance Roadmap

  • Step 1: Upfront Bank Valuation: Order automated property valuation reports across 3–4 lenders.
  • Step 2: Lender Comparison: Select lowest comparison rate and matching loan features (100% offset, credit cards).
  • Step 3: Discharge Authority: Submit formal discharge paperwork to your outgoing bank.
  • Step 4: PEXA Digital Settlement: Automated digital settlement transfers debt seamlessly without bank branch visits.

A couple in Melbourne with a $700,000 mortgage at 6.64% refinanced to a competitive tier-1 lender at 5.99% with an offset account. Their monthly repayments dropped from $4,490 to $4,175, creating $3,780 in net annual cash savings with $0 upfront switching fees.

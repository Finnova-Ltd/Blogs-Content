About EZ Mortgage Broker

We're a team of experienced, passionate mortgage brokers dedicated to simplifying your financial journey and delivering genuinely great outcomes.

Our Story

Built on Trust, Driven by Results

EZ Mortgage Broker was founded with a simple belief: Australians deserve access to unbiased, expert mortgage advice — without the complexity or pressure of dealing directly with banks.

Based in West Perth and serving clients Australia-wide, we've helped thousands of Australians achieve their property goals over more than 15 years. Whether you're a first home buyer, a property investor, or a business owner — we have the expertise and network to make it happen.

As an ASIC-licensed credit provider and member of the MFAA, we're bound by the Best Interest Duty — meaning everything we do is in your best financial interest, not the bank's.

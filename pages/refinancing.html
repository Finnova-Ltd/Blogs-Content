FAQ

Refinancing Questions Answered

What are the costs involved in refinancing? +
Costs typically include a discharge fee from your existing lender ($150–$400), government fees for transferring the mortgage, and potentially break fees if you're on a fixed rate. We'll give you a full cost-benefit analysis before you commit to anything.
How long does the refinancing process take? +
Usually 2–4 weeks from application to settlement. We streamline the process to get you into your new, better-rate loan as quickly as possible.
Will refinancing affect my credit score? +
A new credit enquiry will appear, but a single refinance typically has a minimal impact. We advise on timing to minimise any effect, particularly if you're planning other credit applications.
Can I refinance if I have less than 20% equity? +
Yes, but you may need to pay LMI again on the new loan. We'll calculate whether the savings outweigh the cost, so you can make an informed decision.

FAQ

Investment Loan Questions

What deposit do I need for an investment property? +
Investment loans typically require a minimum 10–20% deposit. However, if you have equity in your existing home, you may be able to use that instead of a cash deposit. Contact us to explore your options.
Should I choose interest-only or principal & interest? +
This depends on your cash flow goals and tax situation. Interest-only loans give you lower repayments and maximise tax deductions but don't reduce your loan balance. We recommend discussing this with your accountant — we can work together with them.
Can I use my home equity to buy an investment property? +
Yes — this is one of the most common strategies. If your home has grown in value, you may have equity available to use as a deposit. We can help you access this equity and structure the investment loan correctly.

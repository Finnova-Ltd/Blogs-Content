FAQ

Common Questions About Buying a Home

How much deposit do I need to buy a home? +
Most lenders require a minimum 5% deposit, but 20% allows you to avoid Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI). First home buyers may access government schemes that allow lower deposits. Contact us to understand your options.
How long does pre-approval take? +
Typically 1–5 business days, depending on the lender and how quickly we receive your documentation. We'll help you get everything ready to speed things up.
What costs are involved in buying a property? +
Beyond the purchase price, expect stamp duty (varies by state), conveyancing fees ($1,000–$2,500), building and pest inspections, and lender fees. We'll provide a full cost breakdown during your consultation.
Should I use a broker or go directly to my bank? +
A broker gives you access to multiple lenders and products, saving you time and often money. Our service is free to you — we're paid by the lender you choose. There's no reason not to use us!

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about switching to Google Workspace through Finnova.

How is the 10% discount applied?

By signing up through our partner link (referworkspace.app.goo.gl/9gmN), your 10% promotional credit is automatically mapped to your billing account for your entire first 12 months.

Can I keep my current business email address?

Yes. Google Workspace connects directly to your existing custom domain (e.g. yourname@company.com.au). Google provides an easy step-by-step setup wizard to transfer your email without downtime.

How does the 14-day free trial work?

You can test Gmail, Google Drive, Docs, and Meet with your team for 14 days without paying anything upfront. If you cancel before day 14, you will never be charged.

Why should I switch from free Gmail or webmail?

A custom domain email builds immediate trust with customers and suppliers. Additionally, Google Workspace gives you 30 GB to 5 TB of secure cloud storage, spam filtering, and a 99.9% uptime SLA backed by Google.

How does Gemini AI help our team?

Gemini is integrated directly into Gmail, Docs, and Slides. It helps you draft professional emails, summarize long meeting transcripts, write grant applications, and create presentations in seconds.

Can I keep my existing email address and domain?

Yes. Google Workspace connects directly to your existing domain name (e.g. yourbusiness.com.au). Google provides simple DNS setup wizards, and your previous emails can be migrated seamlessly.

A custom domain email instantly builds customer trust. Furthermore, Google Workspace eliminates server crashes, spam, and storage bottlenecks with 99.9% guaranteed uptime and bank-grade data security.

Can my remote team collaborate simultaneously?

Yes. Real-time document co-authoring in Docs, Sheets, and Slides allows multiple team members to edit simultaneously without ever emailing attachments back and forth.

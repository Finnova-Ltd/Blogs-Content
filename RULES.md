# 📜 Finnova Ecosystem Global Publishing Rules & Standards (`RULES.md`)

This document establishes the **mandatory, non-negotiable global publishing rules and engagement standards** that MUST be checked and strictly enforced across all Finnova digital properties (`ezmortgagebroker.com.au`, `ezconsultants.com.au`, `procrm.com.au`, `finnova.org.au`, `ezsignature.com`).

---

* **All Articles MUST be Published by `R Bakshi`**:
  * **EZ Mortgage Broker**: `R Bakshi (Principal Mortgage Broker MFAA)` / `authorRole: "Principal Mortgage Broker MFAA"`
  * **EZ Consultants**: `R Bakshi (Principal CRM & AI Solutions Architect)` / `authorRole: "Principal Solutions Architect"`
  * **PRO CRM & Finnova**: `R Bakshi (Principal Enterprise Architect & Cyber Specialist)`
* **Zero Third-Party Authors/Bylines**:
  * **STRICT PROHIBITION**: Never use third-party journalist names, syndication bylines, or scraper author names (e.g., **NEVER** mention "Mina Martin", "Staff Reporter", "Anonymous", or any freelance byline).

---

## 📌 2. Column 2 (Sticky Sidebar) Architecture: Card 1 is Author & Advisor

* **Card #1 in Column 2 MUST ALWAYS be the Author Profile Card**:
  * The **Author / Advisor Card** (`Written by R Bakshi`) must be positioned at **Card #1 in Column 2 (Top of Sticky Sidebar)**.
  * This guarantees that as the user scrolls the article, the primary accredited authority figure, headshot, credentials, and 1-click contact buttons (`📞 Call 1300 ...`, `📅 Book Strategy Session`) remain permanently visible in their eye-line.
* **Col 2 Hierarchy Order**:
  1. **Card 1 (Top)**: Author & Principal Advisor Profile Card (R Bakshi, Photo, MFAA/Specialist badges, Direct Call & Booking CTAs).
  2. **Card 2**: In This Article (Table of Contents anchor links).
  3. **Card 3**: Statutory / Technical Summary Quick Facts.
  4. **Card 4**: Official Government / Regulatory Portal Links.

## 🎯 3. Mandatory Article Engagement Criteria

Plain walls of passive text are strictly prohibited. Every article must actively engage the reader and drive micro-conversions using the following **4 Engagement Pillars**:

1. **Mid-Article Financial Impact Table (Visual Comparison)**:
   * Every advisory must feature a structured comparison table showing tangible dollar savings or metrics (e.g., *Mass-Appraisal Notice vs True Market Evidence: Rates, ESVF, and Land Tax Savings*).
2. **Interactive 1-Click Utilities (Copy / Download / Calculators)**:
   * Provide actionable utility blocks (e.g., *1-Click Copy Objection Statement* with clipboard feedback toast, interactive checklist, or rate calculator links).
3. **Mid-Stream Expert Intercept Banners**:
   * Insert contextual broker/architect advisory callout boxes between core sections (e.g., *"Need 2 to 3 Settled Comparable Sales for Your Street? Let R Bakshi generate a free RP Data / CoreLogic report"*).
4. **Actionable Step-by-Step Breakdowns**:
   * Replace generic paragraphs with numbered visual step badges (`[1]`, `[2]`, `[3]`) and structured statutory grounds.

## ⏰ 5. Explicit Publication Date & Time on All Cards & Articles

* **Mandatory Date + Time Display**:
  * Every article page, card thumbnail, and preview badge **MUST display the exact Publication Date and Time** in Australian Eastern Standard Time (AEST/AEDT).
  * **Format**: `📅 DD-Mon-YYYY · HH:MM AM AEST` (e.g., `📅 02-Sep-2026 · 10:55 AM AEST`).
  * **Zero Placeholders**: **NEVER** display vague phrases like *"Added recently"*, *"Just now"*, or static outdated dates.
  * The top website ticker date **MUST dynamically reflect today's local date**.

---

## 🏷️ 6. Concise, High-Authority Title Standards ($\le 80$ Chars)

* **Strict Character Limit**: Every article title must be **$\le 80$ characters** (cleanly formatted, no awkward word cuts).
* **No Clumsy Boilerplate Wrappers**:
  * Do **NOT** wrap raw headlines with long boilerplate like `"What Does [Headline] Mean for Australian Borrowers?"`.
  * Use authoritative, direct action headlines.

## 1. Strategic Principles & AISEO Architecture

### The Shift: From Flat Blog to Customer Journey Ecosystem
* **Old / Ineffective Model:** Random Blog Post → Thin Factsheet → Dead End.
* **EZ Journey Model:** `Google / ChatGPT / Gemini Query` → `Direct Concise Answer` → `Interactive Calculator` → `Prerequisite Concepts` → `Next Journey Step` → `Commercial Service Page` → `Broker Engagement`.

## 3. Internal Linking & Structural Formula

Every evergreen journey article must strictly follow the **6-Point Linking Engine**:
1. **Breadcrumb:** `Home` → `Journey Hub` → `Stage Article`.
2. **Above-the-Fold Quick Answer:** Direct, concise answer to the query with a direct link to the relevant calculator.
3. **Mid-Article Prerequisite Links (2–4 links):** Contextual links to foundational credit concepts (e.g., LVR, LMI, serviceability buffer, HEM benchmarks).
4. **Next-Step Module:** Exactly **one** explicit recommended forward journey article (e.g., *How Much Deposit Do I Need* &rarr; *First Home Buyer Government Schemes*).
5. **Commercial Service Page Bridge:** Link to the matching EZ conversion page only after the reader's question is answered.
6. **Bottom Module:** Interactive Calculator + MFAA Principal Broker consultation CTA + related articles.
